01Profile

About

I'm a Software Development Engineer IV at Avalara, where I've spent five-plus years designing the connectors that let e-commerce and ERP platforms calculate, report, and prove tax compliance in real time — across Adobe Commerce, PrestaShop, WooCommerce, and now European e-invoicing (ELR).

My focus over the last two years has shifted toward agentic AI: I designed and led an LLM-driven platform that reads a failed e-invoice submission the way an engineer would — forms a hypothesis, checks it against retrieved mandate documentation, proposes a fix, and can even resubmit and self-correct against a live sandbox before a human ever looks at it.

Day to day, that means leading and mentoring the engineers around me as much as it means writing code: running knowledge-sharing sessions on tax-calculation internals and CI/CD, pairing with teammates through technical blockers, and sitting in on client and cross-team calls so a production issue turns into a fix, not just a ticket. I carry the same support load myself, resolving customer escalations directly rather than routing them downstream. That combination has held up over five straight years of performance reviews at Avalara — I've been rated a consistently strong performer every year, on technical ownership, delivery reliability, and how I show up for the team.

Before Avalara, thirteen years shipping web platforms across Tax, E-commerce, Finance, Telecom, and Hospitality, across five companies — from junior developer to team lead. I still write the code myself; I just also draw the blueprint first.

ELR Mandate Builder

Production

An AI agent that diagnoses and fixes broken e-invoice submissions on its own.

React 19NestJSGoogle ADKRAGBullMQPostgreSQL
  • Runs a deterministic analysis pass first, then hands the LLM the error, mapping, and findings to propose a reviewable diff fix with a confidence score.
  • Reverse Diagnosis loop resubmits corrected XML to the live ELR sandbox up to 7 times, then derives the mapping fix from what actually worked.
  • RAG knowledge base synced from Confluence, the ELR API, and SharePoint; hardened against prompt injection with field redaction and an optional pre-flight scan.
